How it works

From sunlight to switch-on
in four steps

  1. 01

    Site survey & design

    Drone mapping, shade analysis and load study. You get a 3D system design and a bankable yield report before spending anything.

  2. 02

    Engineering & approvals

    We handle structural calcs, net-metering applications and utility liaison end-to-end. Zero paperwork on your desk.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Certified crews install in days, not weeks — tier-1 panels, premium inverters, storm-rated mounting and tidy cable runs.

  4. 04

    Monitor & maintain

    Live generation dashboards on your phone, automatic fault alerts and scheduled cleaning keep output at peak for decades.
